# DavidMerry.me — Vision and Project Status

**Last updated:** 2026-08-16

## The long-term vision

DavidMerry.me is David Merry's publishing home and proof of practice: the place
where finance leaders, recruiters, and finance professionals can see a credible
point of view on finance transformation, AI, Power BI, Alteryx, data analytics,
and leadership.

The central idea is **Closing the Gap**: use AI as a thinking and building
partner, then turn agreed logic into deterministic, auditable tools that finance
teams can rely on. The site should demonstrate that idea through practical
writing, evidence-backed case studies, and a clear professional narrative—not a
generic online CV.

## Operating agreement

brand, marketing, content, engineering, and accountability. Each session:

1. Re-state what has been completed and the highest-value next action.
