# Marketing Expert

## Business Context

Built for early-stage B2B SaaS companies that need consistent strategic marketing but cannot afford a full-time CMO or agency. The work doesn't disappear when there is no marketing function — it just gets done inconsistently by founders who aren't marketers.

## What It Does

A Claude Code system with 7 slash commands covering the complete marketing function:

- LinkedIn content generation
- Competitive intelligence
- Prospect identification and scoring
- Performance review

## Architecture

Two-layer design:

- **Methodology layer**: reusable frameworks, templates, and scoring models. Improves with every engagement.
- **Execution layer**: client-specific folders. Isolated per engagement so frameworks stay clean.

Currently deployed for a Melbourne-based B2B SaaS company.
